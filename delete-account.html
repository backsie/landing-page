Delete your Backsie account

Applies to the Backsie app on iOS, Android, and the web (Backsie Inc.).

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Backsie and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Choose whether any community cards you published should be deleted or kept without your name, then confirm.

Deletion starts immediately. You are signed out and the account cannot be used again.

Or ask us by email

If you cannot sign in, email hello@backsie.co from the address on the account with the subject "Delete my Backsie account". We verify the request by replying to that address and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your profile: name, username, gender, age range, relationship status, and profile photo.
  • Your conversations with Backsie, checkpoints, private Insight Cards, saved cards, reactions, and replies.
  • Push notification tokens and analytics tied to your account.

What may be kept

  • Community cards you chose to keep: the published card stays readable with no link to your account or name. Choose "delete" during the steps above to remove them too.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in protected backups until they rotate, at most 35 days.
  • Records we must keep by law: for example purchase records, for as long as the law requires.

Only delete some data

You can delete individual cards, replies, and conversations inside the app without deleting your account. To delete other data, email hello@backsie.co and tell us what to remove.

See the Privacy Policy for full details on how Backsie handles your information.
